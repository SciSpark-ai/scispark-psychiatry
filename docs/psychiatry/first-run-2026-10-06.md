## Overview as running text, two tries, 2026-10-07

Both run from My topics, two-week range, gpt-4o for the overview.

1. Every paper must be cited, no limit on sentences (kept). 42 listed. Depression 14 of 14 papers cited, schizophrenia 14 of 19 (the stored overview was cut at 14 sentences; the cut is now 20), ADHD 9 of 9. Nothing dropped by the checks. But the model wrote one sentence per paper and joined none, so the overview is the list again in sentence form. Some kinds were doubtful: "low dose showed similar outcomes to standard dose" filed as no benefit; "rTMS produced different response trajectories" filed as benefit.
2. The same with a limit on sentences (0.6 per paper, at most 8) (taken out). 24 listed. Schizophrenia: 8 of 15 papers cited. Sentences joined unrelated findings under a wrong kind, for example subclinical hypothyroidism and suicide attempts together with trazodone and sleep as one "mixed" finding. Worse than try 1 on both counts.

Conclusion: the model sorts single papers well enough but does not join them reliably. Joining should be done by code from per-paper answers (kind, treatment, short outcome), not asked of the model.
