# Second real run — 2026-10-06 (commit 92f7a98)

Same watch entries, mode and models. New in this run: places under the cap are shared between watch entries and
the journal pool; the run log keeps the model's own grade; listed papers get an AI headline and takeaway.

**Time.** 134 seconds (run 1: 48). Stage starts seen in the browser: planning 0 s, PubMed search 5.5 s, grading
37 s, list and summaries 95 s. Grading took about 58 s and the summaries about 39 s.

**Tokens** (no cost recorded by the app). Planning 327 in / 749 out (`gpt-4o`); stage A 8 calls, 61,879 in /
5,554 out; summaries 3 calls, 16,534 in / 3,518 out (both `gpt-4o-mini`).

**Stage yields.** Retrieved 553, after topic filter 492, after design gate 421, graded 150 (271 over the cap),
listed 34, lower-confidence 43. 22 queries, none failed, 2 returned nothing. No warnings.

**Grading.** 19 of 150 skipped by the model. Of the 131 graded, the model gave: 4 = 34, 3 = 24, 2 = 11, 1 = 6,
0 = 56. After the quote check: 4 = 15, 3 = 13, 2 = 6, 1 = 4, 0 = 93. **37 of the model's 75 positive grades were
zeroed because the quote was not found in the text.** Two of the four examples looked at were the paper's own
title quoted with a final full stop, which the cleaned title does not have. Fixed after this run (the check now
ignores closing punctuation and the kind of dash or apostrophe); the effect is not yet measured.

**Listed papers.** By entry: Schizophrenia 19, depression × esketamine 11, ADHD × methylphenidate 4 (run 1: 23,
4, 2). By design: meta-analysis 16, systematic review 9, randomized trial 7, observational 2.

**Stability.** Only 7 of the 34 listed papers were also among run 1's 29. The cap now grades a different 150
papers, and the model's grades vary between runs; the two causes were not separated.

**Summaries.** 22 of 34 listed papers got a headline and takeaway that passed the code checks; 12 were dropped
(which check failed is not logged). Of those shown, several headlines are generic ("… analyzed", "… evaluated").
